# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and help keep
the project a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of background or
identity. Harassment, insults, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through GitHub
([@avkcode](https://github.com/avkcode)). Reports are handled confidentially,
and maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions, or ban
participants, who do not follow this code.
